# World expansion plan (draft)

Goal: grow the world from 55 rooms to roughly 240. Brightwater becomes the centre of the map,
with three new cities spread out around it, and every level range gets several areas in different
directions (up to 4) so players have variety. Above it all sit the Planes of the Gods, a 50-room
level 51-60 endgame ending with Lord Zek. Respawn stays at Brightwater.

City names are settled: Hearthdeep, Silverbough and Sunspire. Region names are still placeholders.

## The shape of the world

Brightwater sits in the middle with a road out of each gate. Each direction has a theme and gets
harder the farther you go from town:

| Direction | Theme | Levels | City |
|---|---|---|---|
| North (existing) | Northlands, crypt, swamp, Ironspine, Ashen Peaks | 9-52 | none |
| West (new) | farmland, then the snowy western spur of the Ironspine | 3-22 | **Hearthdeep** (dwarves) |
| South (extended) | meadows and woods, then an ancient elven forest | 1-35 | **Silverbough** (elves) |
| East (new) | dusty road and badlands, then open desert | 8-50 | **Sunspire** (desert traders) |
| Above (new) | the Planes of the Gods, reached from Brightwater's temple | 51-60 | none (endgame) |

Loops, so the world isn't a set of dead ends:
- The Rimefells connect to the Mirefen over a high pass, so the west and north meet.
- The Eldergrove's western edge reaches back to Westmarch.
- The Scorchsands' north edge reaches the Ruins of Elandor, so the east and north meet. The ruins
  become an elven city half swallowed by sand.

Same level range in different places, up to 4 options per range for variety:

## Content that comes with it

- **Monsters:** about 30 new types (51 to ~80), plus 4 new bosses at roughly 21, 26, 33 and 48.
  New rooms also reuse existing monsters where they fit (orcs in the badlands, harpies in the
  Rimefells). All are checked with the balance simulator.
- **Cities:** each has an inn (rest and flavour only, since respawn stays in Brightwater), a smith,
  an outfitter, an alchemist and 2-3 quest givers.
- **Gear:** each city sells its own gear for its level range:
  - Hearthdeep: dwarven heavy armour, axes and hammers, levels 10-24.
  - Silverbough: elven light armour, blades, staves and cloaks, levels 22-36.
  - Sunspire: scimitars, silks, rings and amulets, levels 36-50.

  Brightwater's shops drop to gear up to about level 20, and their higher stock moves to the
  city for its level range, so each city is worth the trip. Stock only moves in the phase that
  builds the receiving city, so nothing is ever unavailable in between. Items players already own
  are unaffected.

  These also fill today's gaps: boots between 16 and 45, amulets between 20 and 50, and gloves
  above 30. That comes to about 40 new items.
- **Quests:** about 10 new ones. Captain Halvard (or another Brightwater NPC) points you to each
  new city when you reach its level range, so players find them naturally. Each city then has its
  own local quests and a boss quest.

## Technical changes (small)

- Split `data/rooms.py` into one file per region (`data/rooms/brightwater.py`, etc.). Everything
  that imports `ROOMS` keeps working.
- Teach `tools/draw_map.py` to draw areas that don't sit on the surface grid (the crypt, the mine,
  the tomb and each plane) as separate insets.
- Old saves are safe. Existing room ids stay as they are, and the world loader already spawns
  rooms that aren't in a save. No changes to respawn or other game code until phase 5.
- Phase 5 adds two gated doors and a saved record of which plane bosses a character has killed
  (a new `Character` field with a default, so old saves still load).

## Build order

Each phase leaves the game fully playable and gets checked with the data check, the map tool, the
balance simulator and a scripted walk-through.

1. **Groundwork and the north (+19 rooms):** split the room files, add multi-inset support to the
   map, then fill out the Ironspine, Elandor, Ashen Peaks, Mirefen and Northlands.
2. **The west (+36):** Westmarch, the Rimefells, Hearthdeep and the Deepdelve mine.
3. **The south (+33):** extend Greenmeadow and the Woods, then the Eldergrove and Silverbough.
4. **The east (+53):** Dustway, the Redrock Badlands, the Scorchsands, Sunspire and the Tomb.
5. **The Planes of the Gods (+50):** the endgame below.

I'd send you one region's rooms to read before writing the rest of each phase.

## Phase 5: the Planes of the Gods (levels 51-60)

The realms of the gods, ruled by Lord Zek, the rightful king of all the gods. The planes are his
trials: each plane's god or guardian tests mortals who climb toward his throne, and beating Lord
Zek means earning his recognition, not overthrowing him. His servants speak of him with reverence.
Anyone can enter the planes. They're built to kill anyone who isn't a many-lives character.

**Getting there.** A stair behind the altar of Brightwater's Temple of the Dawn climbs to a small
hub, the Threshold of the Gods, with a door to each first-tier plane. That puts the way to the
gods at the centre of the world, and Brightwater stays the place every player returns to.

**Layout: 50 rooms in three tiers.** Each tier is reached through the one before it:

| Tier | Plane | Levels | Rooms | Leads on to | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | Threshold of the Gods (hub) | safe | 2 | the three first-tier planes, and the sealed door to Time | |
| 1 | Plane of Sky | 51-54 | 6 | Plane of Air | ~55 |
| 1 | Plane of Growth | 51-54 | 6 | Planes of Earth and Water | ~55 |
| 1 | Plane of Fear | 51-54 | 6 | Plane of Fire | ~55 |
| 2 | Plane of Fire | 54-57 | 6 | | ~58 |
| 2 | Plane of Water | 54-57 | 6 | | ~58 |
| 2 | Plane of Earth | 54-57 | 6 | | ~58 |
| 2 | Plane of Air | 54-57 | 6 | | ~58 |
| 3 | Plane of Time | 57-59 | 6 | Lord Zek's throne | **Lord Zek, 60** |

Each plane has its own element and monsters, so the tiers play differently.

**Two gates:**
- **The door to the Plane of Time**, in the Threshold of the Gods, opens only after the character
  has killed all four elemental lords (Fire, Water, Earth and Air). Every plane gets used on the
  way to the top. The game remembers these kills per character, in the save.
- **The door to Lord Zek's throne** opens only for a character who has lived every class to level
  50. This is a hard check on lived classes, since rebirth allows repeating classes and boss
  tuning alone can't enforce "every class".

Both doors tell the player what they want when they're refused, so the goals are clear.

**Lord Zek.** Tuned with a new endgame mode in the balance simulator, which builds characters with
1, 6 and 11 past lives:
- An 11-class character with the best gear and potions should win most fights, but not every one.
- A 6-life character should almost never win, even with good gear.
- The fight can lean on the multiclass toolkit (heals, stuns, shields, damage over time) that only
  a character with every class has.

**Why players would cross 50 rooms.** XP stops at level 50, so the planes have to pay in other ways:
- Mythic gear: level 50 items stronger than anything else in the game, each plane's boss dropping
  gear in its own style. Items above level 50 could never be equipped.
- Gold, and a trophy or title on the score sheet for killing Lord Zek.
- XP for reborn characters. A player in a later life (say level 42 with 8 past lives) is strong
  enough to fight in the first-tier planes and gets the top XP multiplier there. Those planes
  become a dangerous but fast place to re-level, which shortens the 11-life grind a little.

**Things to watch in tuning:**
- A level 50 player against a level 60 monster deals 0.4x damage and takes 1.4x under the current
  level-gap rule. Past-life stat bonuses are what overcome this, and the simulator will tell us
  whether the curve needs a tweak for this zone.
- Players who keep rebirthing past 11 lives will eventually overpower Lord Zek. That's an intended
  reward for the grind.
- About 20 new monster types, 8 plane bosses plus Lord Zek, and 15-20 mythic items.

## Decisions

- **City names:** Hearthdeep, Silverbough and Sunspire.
- **Lord Zek:** the rightful ruler of the gods. The planes are his trials.
- **Plane of Time:** needs all four elemental lords killed first.
- **Throne door:** the every-class check gates Lord Zek only. All other planes are open to anyone
  who can survive them.
- **Brightwater's shops:** keep gear up to about level 20; higher gear moves to the new cities.
- **Areas per level range:** up to 4, for variety.
- **Respawn:** always Brightwater.
